## Visual direction

- **Mood:** instrument craft / serious builder portfolio  
- **Atmosphere:** deep charcoal + paper grain / soft vignette (not flat void)  
- **Type:** IBM Plex Serif / Sans / Mono  
- **Accents:** Signal Orange `#F47A24` (warm brand); Panel Blue `#4BA4C7` (proof/links)  
- **Motion:** hero atmosphere, proof focus, section reveal (2–3 total)  
- **Avoid:** purple-glow AI defaults, cream+terracotta cliché, broadsheet density, hero cards/chips, dashboard chrome
